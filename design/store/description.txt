Most food apps hand you a feed. Food & Friends hands you a journal.

Write down the recipes you actually cook, plan the week's menus around them, and walk into the store with a list that already knows what's on the shelf. Then pull up a chair at The Table and see what your friends have been making. Set like a printed cookbook: warm paper, serif type, hairline rules, and nothing shouting at you.

RECIPES
Write a recipe down the way you'd write it on a card — ingredients, method, a photo of how it turned out. Every recipe goes into your box, and each one is either kept among friends or shared with everyone. Your call, recipe by recipe.

VARIATIONS AND NOTES
Nobody makes a recipe the same way twice. Keep your variations and notes on a recipe — less sugar, a longer bake, the swap that worked — so the next time is the better time.

MENUS
Plan the week's menu, a recipe to a day, and see at a glance what's for dinner on Thursday.

THE SHOPPING LIST
Add a recipe's ingredients to the shopping list and each line remembers which recipe it's for. Add your own lines too. Tick things into the cart as you go, then clear it and start fresh.

YOUR KROGER STORE
Pick a Kroger-family store by ZIP code and match the lines on your list to real products on its shelves, with prices and sale tags. No Kroger account needed.

THE TABLE
Add friends by username. When they write down a recipe or plan a menu, it turns up at The Table, and yours turns up at theirs. A small table, by invitation — not a feed of strangers.

A FRIENDLY TABLE
Report any recipe, menu, entry or person that doesn't belong, and block anyone you'd rather not see. Reports are reviewed, and objectionable content comes down.

ALSO IN THE BOOK
- Sign in with Apple, Google, or email
- Light and dark, like a kitchen at noon and at midnight
- Delete your account and everything in it from inside the app
- No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold
