Thank you for reviewing Food & Friends.

Food & Friends is a cook's journal. You write down the recipes you cook (with photos), keep variations and notes on them, plan the week's menus, and keep a shopping list that can be matched to products at a nearby Kroger store. Friends can see each other's activity at The Table, a shared feed of what everyone has been cooking.

The account is fully set up — there is no waitlist, invite code, or manual approval step. It already contains recipes (one with a photo), a weekly menu, a shopping list, and a friend, so The Table has entries to display.

Email and password, Sign in with Apple, and Google Sign-In are all supported on the sign-in screen. New accounts accept the Terms of Use when they sign up.

The chapters are along the bottom of every screen: HOME · RECIPES · SHOPPING, and MORE for MENUS · TABLE · YOU.

KROGER
On the Shopping list, the user can pick a Kroger store by ZIP code (or by pasting a kroger.com store link) and match shopping-list items to products and prices. This uses Kroger's public product API, called directly from the device. It works with any US ZIP code that has a Kroger-family store nearby (for example 45202, Cincinnati, OH). No Kroger account is needed or linked, and nothing is purchased in the app.

LOCATION
Location is optional. The app asks for it only if the user taps "Find me" during profile setup, to fill in a city and state once; the coordinates are not stored. Typing a city or leaving it blank works just as well.

ACCOUNT DELETION
In-app account deletion is under You → Delete account. It permanently deletes the profile, recipes, variations, menus, shopping lists, activity, friend links, photos and the sign-in account.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT AND MODERATION
Users can write recipes and menus, and friends see each other's activity at The Table. Each recipe is either kept among friends or shared with everyone.
- Report an entry: press and hold an entry at The Table → Report.
- Report a recipe or a person: the ⋯ menu on the recipe, or on the person's profile → Report.
- Block a person: from their profile → Block. Blocking ends the friendship and hides their content.
- Every new account accepts the Terms of Use at sign-up, which state zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
Reports are reviewed by the developer; objectionable content is removed and repeat offenders' accounts are removed, typically within 24 hours.
