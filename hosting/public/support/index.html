Support

A cook's journal, for iPhone

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, ideas, or anything else about Food & Friends: email david.lonneman@gmail.com. Most messages get an answer within a couple of days. If something went wrong, it helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you're on, and what you were doing at the time.

Common questions

How do I sign in?
Use your email and a password, Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with Google, all on the first screen. New accounts pick a username next; that's how friends find you. If you started with Apple or Google, use the same button each time so you land in the same account.
I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?", enter the email you signed up with, and we'll send a link to set a new one. If you signed up with Apple or Google, there's no separate password: sign in with that button again.
How do I add friends?
Open You → Friends and find a friend by their username. They get a note asking them to your table; once they accept, you'll see each other's activity at The Table and the recipes you've kept among friends.
Who can see my recipes?
You choose for each recipe: kept among friends (you and your friends), or shared with everyone. Your shopping list is yours alone.
How do I report something, or block someone?
To report an entry at The Table, press and hold it and choose Report. To report a recipe or a person, use the ⋯ menu on the recipe or on their profile. To block someone, open their profile and choose Block. Blocking ends your friendship and hides their content from you. Every report is reviewed, usually within 24 hours. Objectionable content is removed, and people who post it lose their accounts. You can also email david.lonneman@gmail.com.
How does the Kroger store work?
On the Shopping list, pick a Kroger store by ZIP code (any US ZIP with a Kroger-family store nearby), or paste the link to your store's page from kroger.com. Then tap a line to pick its product, or match them all at once, to see what's on the shelf and what it costs there. You don't need a Kroger account. Prices and availability come straight from Kroger and can be out of date, so check at the store.
Why does the app ask for my location?
Only if you tap "Find me" while setting up your profile. It fills in your city and state once, and your exact location isn't stored. You can type your city instead, or leave it blank.

Deleting your account

Open You → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile, recipes, variations, menus, shopping lists, activity, friend links, photos, and your sign-in account. It can't be undone. If you'd rather we handle it, email david.lonneman@gmail.com from the address on the account.
